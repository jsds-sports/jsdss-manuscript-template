# Note to JSDSS Associate Editors about review process

Dear JSDSS AE,

Thanks again for your willingness to be an Associate Editor for the Journal of Statistics and Data Science in Sports (JSDSS).
We greatly appreciate your efforts as we build JSDSS.  

As we begin to receive submissions, we want to share our plan for integrating our Associate Editors (AEs) into the review process.
One of the distinct elements of JSDSS is that we expect each submission to be reproducible.  
We have developed a detailed overview of the manuscript review process including the reproducibility assessment which will happen via github.
Here's a link to the [full review process](https://github.com/jsds-sports/jsdss-manuscript-template/blob/main/instructions-all.md).
In short, a submission will start with a typical review of content, and only after the reviewers and the AE, are convinced that the submission is credible for publication will the manuscript proceed to the reproducibility/github review.  
An invitation to GitHub review is not a conditional acceptance. 
Comments from reviewers to the authors *may* be made public and attributable to the reviewer by name, if the manuscript goes through to the github review.  
Comments to the editors will *never* be public.

During this initial period of the journal, we are planning to have you serve as a reviewer for one or manuscripts so that you can have a thorough understanding of the review process.  
Thus, in the near term you may be invited to serve as a reviewer for a JSDSS article rather than as an AE.  
Please take this as an opportunity to learn the review process as well as the familiarize yourself with OJS which is the management system hosted by UNC Charlotte Libraries.
You should have an account for JSDSS that was set up for you recently.  

If you have questions about any of this, please don't hesitate to reach out.
